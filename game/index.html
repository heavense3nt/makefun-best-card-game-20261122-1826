EMBER CLASH

A tactical card battler. Outdraw the Cinder Warden,
spend your embers wisely, and take the table.

How to play

  • Both fighters start at 30 life. Reduce the Warden to 0 to win.
  • Each turn you gain energy (1, then 2, … up to 10) and draw a card.
  • Click a card — or press 1–7 — to play it. Each card costs energy.
  • ▲ Flame deals damage · ❄ Frost damages and shields · ❦ Verdant heals · ◆ Stone shields · ✦ Spark draws cards.
  • Shield soaks damage first, then halves at the start of your turn. Burn ticks at the start of a turn.
  • Press Enter to end your turn. The Warden plays after you.
